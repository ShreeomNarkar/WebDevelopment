Flamingo

The Greater Flamingo is the largest and most widespread species of flamingo, found in Africa, southern Europe, the Middle East, and South Asia. Known for their tall, slender bodies and striking pink plumage, they thrive in shallow saltwater lagoons and wetlands. Their diet mainly consists of algae, plankton, and crustaceans, which give them their pink color. Highly social, they live in large flocks and perform synchronized group displays during breeding. They are also famous for their unique one-legged stance and specialized beak, which helps them filter-feed in the water.
